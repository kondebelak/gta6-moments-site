Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who this applies to

"GTA6 Moments Publisher" is an internal tool used only to publish clips to the GTA6 Moments accounts. The only account that signs in to it is our own.

What data we access

When our account authorizes the tool on TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, the platform gives the tool an access token and basic profile information of that account (such as the account ID, username and avatar). With the permissions we grant, the tool uploads our videos and reads the view and engagement statistics of our own posts.

How we use it

Only to publish our clips and to measure how they perform. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising.

Viewers and other users

The tool does not collect any data about people who watch our videos, and it does not collect data from any other TikTok, YouTube or Instagram user.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored by our integration provider (Composio) and in our own private systems, and are never published. Performance statistics are kept in a private database.

Deletion

We can revoke the tool's access at any time from the platform's settings, which stops all data access. Contact us to ask about any data we hold.

Contact

Send a direct message to @the.gta6.moments on Instagram.
